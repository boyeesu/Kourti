## 1. Introduction and Purpose

This document is the Sub-processor Register ("Register") for Kourti Legal. It lists all third-party sub-processors that Kourti engages, or may engage, to process Customer Personal Data in connection with the Kourti Legal platform.

This Register is maintained pursuant to Clause 6 of Kourti's [Data Processing Agreement](./DPA.md) and in accordance with the requirements of:

- GDPR Article 28(2) and (3);
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) Section 29; and
- Applicable NITDA/NDPC guidance on sub-processing.

For the definition of "Customer Personal Data", "Sub-processor", and other defined terms used in this Register, please refer to Section 2 of the DPA.

---

## 2. How Customers Are Notified of Changes

Kourti manages changes to this Register as follows:

1. **Email notification:** When Kourti intends to add, replace, or remove a Sub-processor, it will send an email notification to the Customer's registered administrator email address at least **30 calendar days** before the change takes effect.

2. **Register update:** The Register will be updated with the proposed change, marked with a "[PENDING — effective: DD Month YYYY]" flag, so that Customers can review the change via this document at any time.

3. **Right to object:** Customers may object to an intended change within 30 calendar days of the notification date, on reasonable, documented data protection grounds. Objections should be sent to: **privacy@kourti.legal** (or the address specified in the DPA). If an objection cannot be resolved, the Customer may terminate the affected service in accordance with the DPA.

4. **Acceptance:** Failure to object within the 30-day period constitutes acceptance of the change.

5. **Emergency changes:** In exceptional circumstances (e.g., a Sub-processor becomes insolvent or poses an active security risk), Kourti may engage a replacement Sub-processor on shorter notice, in which case Kourti will notify the Customer as promptly as possible and document the reasons.

## 4. Notes on AI Sub-processor Data Flows

Given the sensitivity of legal data processed through the Kourti platform, the following additional notes apply to AI/LLM Sub-processors (entries 2, 3, and 4 above):

- Customer Personal Data submitted to AI features (including document text, matter details, and client names) is transmitted to the relevant AI inference Sub-processor **transiently for inference only** and is not persistently stored by the Sub-processor beyond the operational requirements of serving the inference request, subject to each Sub-processor's data retention policies as documented in their DPAs.
- Kourti contractually prohibits each AI Sub-processor from using Customer Personal Data for training, fine-tuning, evaluation, or improvement of AI models.
- Customers who wish to avoid submitting particular data to AI Sub-processors should use the relevant feature exclusion settings within the Kourti platform, where available.
- Data sent through OpenRouter (entry 4) may be routed to underlying model providers beyond Anthropic and OpenAI. Kourti will maintain an up-to-date list of model providers accessible via OpenRouter upon request.
