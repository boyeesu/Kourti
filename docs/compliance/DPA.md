## 1. Background and Scope

1.1 Kourti provides an AI-powered legal practice management platform (the "**Service**") to law firms and legal professionals.

1.2 In providing the Service, Kourti processes Personal Data on behalf of the Customer. The Customer, as a law firm, acts as the **Data Controller** in respect of the Personal Data of its own clients, staff, opposing parties, and other individuals whose data it inputs into or generates through the Service. Kourti acts as the **Data Processor** in respect of that Personal Data.

1.3 This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") governs the rights and obligations of each Party with respect to the processing of such Personal Data and forms part of, and is incorporated into, the Kourti Legal Terms of Service or the applicable Order Form (the "**Main Agreement**"). In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Main Agreement on data protection matters, this DPA prevails.

1.4 This DPA is intended to comply with:

- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "**GDPR**") and, where applicable, the UK GDPR;
- The Nigeria Data Protection Regulation 2019 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (collectively, "**NDPR/NDPA**"); and
- Any other applicable data protection legislation in force during the term of the Main Agreement.

## 3. Subject Matter, Nature, Duration, and Purpose of Processing

### 3.1 Subject Matter

Kourti processes Customer Personal Data solely for the purpose of providing the Service to the Customer under the Main Agreement, including:

- Hosting and storing legal matter files, documents, and communications;
- Running AI analysis, summarization, drafting assistance, and document review on uploaded materials;
- Generating automated notifications, reminders, and portal updates;
- Enabling Client Portal access for the law firm's own clients; and
- Facilitating internal collaboration tools for the Customer's staff.

### 3.2 Nature of Processing

Storage, retrieval, structuring, analysis (including AI/LLM inference), transmission, and display of Customer Personal Data within the Service.

### 3.3 Duration

Kourti will process Customer Personal Data for the duration of the Main Agreement and, thereafter, for such period as is necessary to fulfil any statutory retention obligations or to comply with Clause 11 (Return and Deletion), whichever is shorter.

### 3.4 Purpose

Processing is carried out solely for the purpose of delivering the features and functionality of the Kourti Legal platform as described in the Main Agreement and applicable documentation.

## 4. Categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data

### 4.1 Categories of Data Subjects

Customer Personal Data processed under this DPA may relate to the following categories of Data Subjects:

1. **Law firm staff** — solicitors, associates, paralegals, support staff, and other personnel of the Customer who use the Service;
2. **The Customer's clients** — individuals and corporate representatives whose legal matters are managed through the platform;
3. **Opposing parties and third parties** — individuals named in legal documents, contracts, correspondence, or matter notes uploaded to the platform;
4. **Third-party contacts** — witnesses, experts, counterparties, and other individuals referenced in uploaded materials.

### 4.2 Categories of Personal Data

The following categories of Personal Data may be processed:

- **Identification data:** Full names, national identification numbers, passport numbers, dates of birth;
- **Contact data:** Email addresses, postal addresses, telephone numbers;
- **Professional data:** Job titles, employer names, bar registration numbers, professional licences;
- **Legal matter data:** Case names, matter references, legal documents (contracts, pleadings, evidence, correspondence), legal advice, litigation strategy;
- **Financial data:** Bank account details, payment amounts, fee arrangements, financial disclosures in legal proceedings;
- **Authentication data:** Hashed passwords, MFA credentials (processed transiently; not stored in recoverable form);
- **Special categories of data (potentially):** Legal documents uploaded by the Customer may incidentally contain health data, biometric data, criminal offence data, or other special category data as defined under GDPR Article 9. The Customer, as Controller, is responsible for determining the lawful basis for any such processing and for notifying Kourti if the volume of special category data warrants additional safeguards.

## 5. Processor Obligations

### 5.1 Process Only on Instructions

5.1.1 Kourti shall process Customer Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Customer, including as set out in this DPA and the Main Agreement, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law. In such a case, Kourti shall, to the extent permitted by law, inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing.

5.1.2 The Customer's use of the Service configuration, user permissions, and feature selections within the platform constitutes documented instructions for the purposes of this Clause.

5.1.3 Kourti shall promptly inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data protection law.

### 5.2 Confidentiality

5.2.1 Kourti shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

5.2.2 Kourti shall not disclose Customer Personal Data to any third party except as expressly permitted under this DPA or the Main Agreement, or as required by law.

### 5.3 Security

5.3.1 Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing, as well as the risks of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of Data Subjects, Kourti shall implement and maintain appropriate Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs) to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as further described in **Annex 2** of this DPA.

5.3.2 The TOMs in Annex 2 represent the minimum standard. Kourti may update the TOMs from time to time to reflect improvements in security technology and practice, provided that the overall level of security is not reduced.

### 5.4 Assistance with Data Subject Rights

5.4.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, Kourti shall assist the Customer by implementing appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, for the fulfilment of the Customer's obligation to respond to requests for exercising Data Subjects' rights under Chapter III of the GDPR and Part IV of the NDPA, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection.

5.4.2 Kourti shall forward any request received directly from a Data Subject to the Customer without undue delay and shall not respond to such a request except on the documented instructions of the Customer.

5.4.3 Where Kourti is unable to provide data export or deletion functionality through the Service interface, Kourti shall cooperate with the Customer to fulfil such requests within the timescales required by applicable law.

### 5.5 Assistance with Compliance Obligations

Kourti shall assist the Customer in ensuring compliance with the obligations pursuant to GDPR Articles 32 to 36 and equivalent NDPA obligations, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to Kourti, including:

- Security of processing (Article 32 / NDPA Section 24);
- Notification of a Personal Data Breach to the Supervisory Authority (Article 33 / NDPA Section 40);
- Communication of a Personal Data Breach to Data Subjects (Article 34 / NDPA Section 40);
- Data Protection Impact Assessments (Article 35 / NDPA Section 26); and
- Prior consultation with the Supervisory Authority (Article 36 / NDPA Section 27).

### 5.6 Audit Rights

5.6.1 Kourti shall make available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor mandated by the Customer.

5.6.2 The Customer shall give Kourti reasonable notice of no less than **30 calendar days** before commencing an audit. Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours, no more than once per calendar year (unless a Personal Data Breach has occurred), and in a manner that minimises disruption to Kourti's operations. The Customer shall bear the costs of any third-party auditor engaged by it.

5.6.3 Kourti may satisfy its audit obligations by providing the Customer with relevant third-party audit reports (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001 certification) or security questionnaire responses, subject to the Customer executing any applicable confidentiality undertaking.

5.6.4 The Customer shall treat all information obtained during an audit as Confidential Information subject to the terms of the Main Agreement.

## 8. Personal Data Breach Notification

### 8.1 Discovery and Internal Response

Upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, Kourti shall:

1. Immediately activate its internal incident response procedure;
2. Take all necessary steps to contain and mitigate the breach; and
3. Preserve evidence and document the breach in its internal incident log.

### 8.2 Notification to Customer

8.2.1 Kourti shall notify the Customer of a Personal Data Breach **without undue delay** and, where feasible, **no later than 48 hours** after becoming aware of the breach. Where notification cannot be made within 48 hours, Kourti shall provide an initial notification with the information available at that time, followed by further notifications as additional information becomes available.

8.2.2 The initial notification shall include, to the extent available at the time:

- A description of the nature of the breach, including (where possible) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected and the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records affected;
- The name and contact details of Kourti's data protection point of contact from whom more information can be obtained;
- A description of the likely consequences of the breach; and
- A description of the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

  8.2.3 Kourti shall cooperate with the Customer in providing all further information and assistance necessary to enable the Customer to fulfil its obligations to notify Supervisory Authorities and Data Subjects under Article 33 and 34 of the GDPR and Section 40 of the NDPA.

### 8.3 Customer Responsibilities

The Customer remains solely responsible for notifying the relevant Supervisory Authority and affected Data Subjects in accordance with applicable law. Kourti's breach notification to the Customer does not constitute an admission of fault or liability.

## 9. Return and Deletion of Data

### 9.1 On Termination or Expiry

Upon termination or expiry of the Main Agreement, or upon written request by the Customer, Kourti shall, at the Customer's election:

- **Return:** Export and make available to the Customer a complete copy of all Customer Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (JSON, CSV, or equivalent) within **30 calendar days**; **or**
- **Delete:** Securely delete or destroy all Customer Personal Data (including copies held by Sub-processors, where technically feasible) within **30 calendar days**, subject to Clause 9.2.

  9.1.2 Where the Customer does not make an election within 30 days of termination, Kourti shall delete Customer Personal Data in accordance with its standard data retention schedule.

### 9.2 Legal Retention Obligations

Kourti may retain Customer Personal Data for a longer period to the extent required by applicable law (including financial, tax, and regulatory record-keeping obligations), provided that:

- The data is retained only for the period strictly required by the applicable obligation;
- The data is kept separate from active Customer data and access is restricted; and
- Kourti notifies the Customer of any such retention obligation and the expected retention period.

### 9.3 Certification

Upon completion of deletion, Kourti shall, upon request, provide the Customer with written certification that all Customer Personal Data has been deleted or returned.

## Annex 2 — Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs)

The following TOMs represent the minimum security standards maintained by Kourti in respect of Customer Personal Data as at the Effective Date. Kourti may update these measures from time to time to reflect improvements in security technology, provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

### A. Access Control and Authentication

| Measure                               | Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Password hashing**                  | All user passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a work factor (cost) of 12 before storage. Plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.                                                                                                          |
| **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** | Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) MFA is available for all user accounts and is enforced for administrator-level accounts. MFA seed secrets are stored encrypted.                                                                                 |
| **Session management**                | Sessions use cryptographically random tokens. Sessions expire after inactivity. Concurrent session controls are applied per account.                                                                                                                |
| **Role-based access control (RBAC)**  | Access to platform features and data is controlled through granular capability-based roles (including `superadmin`, `support`, and `billing` tiers at the platform level). Users are granted least-privilege access consistent with their function. |
| **Tenant isolation**                  | Customer Personal Data is logically isolated at the database level by mandatory `organization_id` scoping on all relevant queries and API endpoints. Cross-tenant data access is architecturally prevented.                                         |
| **Admin access controls**             | Platform administrative access follows least-privilege principles. Privileged actions (including impersonation) are logged and subject to capability gating.                                                                                        |

### B. Cryptography and Data Integrity

| Measure                   | Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Encryption in transit** | All data in transit between clients and the Kourti platform is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. HTTP connections are redirected to HTTPS.                                                                               |
| **Encryption at rest**    | Customer Personal Data stored in the PostgreSQL database and file storage is encrypted at rest using infrastructure-level encryption provided by the hosting provider (Railway).                                          |
| **Signed file URLs**      | Access to stored files (documents, attachments) is controlled via HMAC-signed, time-limited URLs. URLs expire after a short period and are not reusable. Direct, unauthenticated access to file storage is not permitted. |

### C. Data Integrity and Malware Controls

| Measure              | Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Malware scanning** | All files uploaded to the platform are scanned for malware using ClamAV before being made available to users. Files that fail scanning are quarantined and not stored.                                                                                |
| **Audit logging**    | Significant actions (login, data access, document upload/download, matter creation/deletion, administrative actions) are recorded in a tamper-evident audit log with timestamp and user identity. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 24 months. |
| **Data validation**  | Input validation and parameterized queries are used throughout the application to prevent injection attacks and data corruption.                                                                                                                      |

### D. Availability and Resilience

| Measure                         | Implementation                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rate limiting**               | API endpoints are rate-limited to protect against denial-of-service attacks and credential stuffing.                                        |
| **Infrastructure availability** | The Service is hosted on Railway's managed infrastructure, which provides built-in redundancy and automated failover for database services. |
| **Backups**                     | Database backups are performed on a scheduled basis by the infrastructure provider. Backup restoration procedures are tested periodically.  |

### E. Organizational Measures

| Measure                         | Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Staff training**              | Personnel with access to Customer Personal Data receive data protection awareness training on joining and periodically thereafter.                                                              |
| **Confidentiality obligations** | All staff and contractors with access to Customer Personal Data are subject to written confidentiality obligations.                                                                             |
| **Vendor due diligence**        | Sub-processors are subject to security and data protection due diligence before engagement and periodically during the relationship.                                                            |
| **Incident response**           | Kourti maintains a documented Personal Data Breach response procedure, including internal escalation paths, containment steps, and notification workflows consistent with Clause 8 of this DPA. |
| **Data protection by design**   | Data minimization, purpose limitation, and access control principles are incorporated into the design and development of platform features.                                                     |
