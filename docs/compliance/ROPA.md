## Roles Clarification

Kourti acts in **two distinct capacities** depending on the activity:

| Capacity            | When                                                                       | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Data Controller** | Own users, marketing leads, billing, analytics, platform administration    | Kourti determines the purpose and means of processing                                                                                                                                 |
| **Data Processor**  | Firm client/matter data (clients, cases, documents, AI processing, portal) | The subscribing law firm is the Controller; Kourti processes on its instructions under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA). A DPA template must be in place with each subscribing firm. |

Where Kourti is a **Processor**, the subscribing law firm bears primary Controller obligations under GDPR Art. 28 and NDPR. Kourti's own obligations are governed by the DPA.
