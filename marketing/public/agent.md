## Product — What Kourti Legal Does

Kourti Legal is an **AI-powered legal practice management platform** that helps lawyers and legal teams manage their entire workflow in one place. It replaces fragmented tools — spreadsheets, email chains, physical filing cabinets, and separate billing software — with a single unified platform.

### Core Value Proposition

- **One platform for every legal task** — matters, contracts, deadlines, documents, clients, and analytics unified in one place.
- **AI-native** — AI summarizes documents, flags risk, extracts key clauses, transcribes meetings, and automates reminders. It's not a bolt-on; AI is built into every workflow.
- **Built for African and global law practices** — designed with real legal workflows in mind, not generic project management tools repackaged for law firms.
- **Built by former lawyers** — the founding team spent years in active legal practice and built Kourti to solve the problems they lived through: clunky software, missed deadlines buried in emails, hours lost on administrative tasks.

### What Problems Does Kourti Solve?

1. **Time wasted on document review** — AI summarizes contracts and flags risk in minutes instead of hours.
2. **Missed deadlines** — Smart reminders monitor every deadline and push alerts before things become urgent. No more relying on manual diaries.
3. **Lost document versions** — Track every version, redline changes, and manage approvals without email chaos or lost edits.
4. **Fragmented client information** — All client profiles, matters, documents, notes, and communication history live in one centralized hub.
5. **No visibility into firm performance** — Practice analytics show where time goes, track matter progress, and identify bottlenecks.
6. **Scaling support without scaling costs** — AI handles repetitive tasks so teams can focus on high-value legal work.
7. **Security concerns** — SOC 2 compliant with end-to-end encryption and bank-grade data protection.

## Detailed Feature Breakdown

### 1. AI Document Analysis

Upload any contract or legal document. Kourti's AI reads the document and returns:

- A **plain-language summary** of the document
- **Key clauses** extracted and highlighted
- **Risk flags** identifying potentially problematic terms or missing provisions
- Results delivered in **minutes, not hours**

This works on all common legal document formats including PDFs, Word documents, and more.

### 2. Smart Contract Management

Manage the entire contract lifecycle:

- **Version tracking** — see every edit, who made it, and when
- **Redlining** — track changes and compare versions side by side
- **Approval workflows** — manage sign-off processes without email chaos
- **AI-powered contract creation** (Firm plan and above) — generate draft contracts with AI assistance
- **AI risk analysis & redlining** (Firm plan and above) — automated risk assessment during contract review

### 3. Matter Management

Manage all your legal matters (cases) with:

- **Deadline tracking** — never miss a filing date or court appearance
- **Task management** — assign and track tasks within each matter
- **Customizable fields** — configure fields your practice actually needs
- **Unlimited matters** on the Firm plan (Solo plan supports up to 8 active matters)

### 4. Smart Reminders

AI-powered deadline monitoring:

- Watches all your deadlines across matters
- Pushes **alerts before things become urgent**
- Automated reminders for filing dates, renewals, and client follow-ups
- Available as automated reminders on the Firm plan and above

### 5. Client Hub

A centralized place for everything related to each client:

- **Client profiles** with contact details and history

- **Documents** — all client-related files in one place
- **Notes** — internal notes and memos
- **Communication history** — track all interactions
- **Client portal access** (Firm plan and above) — give clients secure access to their matter updates

### 6. Practice Analytics

Understand how your firm operates:

Kourti Legal was born in the trenches of legal practice. The founding team spent years in active practice navigating the chaos of traditional legal work: juggling spreadsheets for matter tracking, hunting through endless email threads for document versions, and manually monitoring deadlines that should never be missed.

They asked: why should legal professionals — some of the most highly trained minds in the world — spend so much time on administrative work that technology could handle better?

So they built Kourti Legal. Not as a generic SaaS platform with legal features bolted on, but as a purpose-built solution designed from day one for lawyers, by former lawyers. Every feature, every workflow, every AI capability was crafted with deep understanding of how legal work actually happens.

Today, legal teams across the globe use Kourti to reclaim their time, reduce risk, and deliver better outcomes for their clients.

4. **Built for Teams** — From solo practitioners to large firms, the platform scales with your practice without sacrificing simplicity.

### What Makes Kourti Different

## Legal Tech Readiness Assessment

Kourti offers a free **Practice Technology Maturity Assessment** available at [kourti.com/assessment](https://kourti.com/assessment). This tool helps law firms evaluate their technology readiness across six dimensions:

### Assessment Dimensions

1. **Legal Research Tools** — How the firm conducts legal research (physical books vs. AI-powered tools)
2. **Document Management** — How case files and documents are stored and organized
3. **Court Filing & Compliance** — How filings and regulatory compliance are tracked
4. **AI Adoption** — The firm's current stance on and use of AI tools
5. **Cybersecurity & Data Protection** — How client data and confidential information are protected
6. **Practice & Client Management** — How matters, billing, and client communication are handled

### Scoring Tiers

- **Explorer (10-17 points):** Relies heavily on traditional methods; significant opportunity for improvement
- **Adopter (18-24 points):** Started digitizing some workflows; key areas still manual
- **Leader (25-32 points):** Solid technology foundations; fine-tuning with AI would put you ahead of 90% of practices
- **Innovator (33-40 points):** Among the most tech-forward practices; continue pushing boundaries

### Sample Assessment Questions

- "How does your firm currently conduct legal research?" (Physical law reports → AI-powered research tools)
- "How does your firm store and organize case files?" (Physical cabinets → Cloud DMS with version control)
- "What is your firm's current stance on AI tools in legal work?" (Not considered → Legal-specific AI in daily workflows)
- "How does your firm protect client data?" (Basic passwords → Comprehensive security with 2FA, encryption, NDPA compliance)

The assessment provides personalized recommendations based on scores in each dimension.
