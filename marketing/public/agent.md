## Company

Kourti Legal is an open-source legal practice workspace owned by Navigi. It is designed for legal professionals who need one place to manage matters, clients, documents, tasks, and deadlines.

- Website: https://kourti.com
- Source code: https://github.com/boyeesu/Kourti
- Parent company: Navigi — https://navigi.io
- Headquarters: Lagos, Nigeria
- Support: support@kourti.com
- General enquiries: info@kourti.com
- Guided demos: https://cal.com/kourti-legal/discovery

Kourti is a technology product. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. AI-generated outputs should be reviewed by qualified legal professionals.

## Product

Kourti brings the operating work of a legal practice into one focused workspace. Core capabilities include:

- Matter management for tasks, files, notes, people, and activity
- Client records connected to related matters and documents
- Contract and document review with summaries, clause extraction, and risk flags
- Deadline, hearing, renewal, and follow-up tracking
- Meeting transcription with decisions and next actions
- Team reminders and practice visibility
- Security controls including encryption, role-based access, and tenant isolation

## How it works

1. Create a matter, add the client, and upload existing files.
2. Review documents, assign tasks, and keep decisions attached to the matter.
3. Track deadlines and follow-ups before they become urgent.

## Open-source availability

Kourti is open source. The repository is available at https://github.com/boyeesu/Kourti. There is no pricing page or free-trial offer on the marketing website. Teams that want a guided walkthrough can book a demo at https://cal.com/kourti-legal/discovery.
