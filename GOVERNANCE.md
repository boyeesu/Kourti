# Governance

Kourti Legal is maintained by the Kourti Legal maintainers.

Maintainers set the roadmap, review pull requests, manage releases, and make
final decisions when consensus cannot be reached. Contributions are evaluated
on technical quality, security, user impact, maintenance cost, and alignment
with the project's goals.

Anyone may propose changes through Issues, Discussions, and pull requests.
Active contributors may be invited to become maintainers based on sustained,
responsible contributions and community conduct. Maintainer access may be
removed to protect project security or community health.
